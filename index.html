I'm new enough to be thrilled when a program does what I told it to.

Hi, I'm Abinavsudhan. I'm in my first year of B.Tech Information Technology, and everything about it is still fresh: the first program that compiled, the first page that loaded, the first bug that took an evening to find.

What pulled me in wasn't a big plan. It was curiosity about what sits behind the screen: why a button responds, why a page loads, who decides how a website looks. Every answer handed me two more questions, and I'm still following them.

I learn best by making things, even small, slightly broken ones. This site is one of them. I set it like a Swiss poster because I like it when everything has a reason to be where it is.

If you're a student, a mentor, or someone with an idea that needs one more pair of hands, I'd like to hear from you.
