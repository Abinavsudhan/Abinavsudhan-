About

The origin story

Hi, I'm Abinavsudhan. I'm in my first year of B.Tech Information Technology, which means I'm still new enough to be thrilled when a program does exactly what I told it to.

What pulled me in wasn't a big plan. It was curiosity about what sits behind the screen: why a button responds, why a page loads, who decides how a website looks. Every answer I found handed me two more questions, and I'm still following them.

I learn best by making things, even small, slightly broken ones. This site is one of them. I made it a comic book because I'd rather you smile when it opens than think you've seen it before.

If you're a student, a mentor, or someone with an idea that needs one more pair of hands, I'd like to hear from you.
