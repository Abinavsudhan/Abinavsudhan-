Hi, I'm Abinavsudhan. I'm in my first year of B.Tech Information Technology, and everything about it is still fresh: the first program that compiled, the first page that loaded, the first bug that ate an entire evening.

What pulled me in wasn't a big plan. It was curiosity about what sits behind the screen: why a button responds, why a page loads, who decides how a website looks. Every answer handed me two more questions, and I'm still following them.

I learn best by making things, even small, slightly broken ones. This site is one of them. I wrote it as a character sheet because I like the idea that you can see where someone is now and where they're headed, honestly, on one card.

If you have a quest that needs one more pair of hands, I'd like to hear about it.
